==========================================================================
INTERPRETATION
==========================================================================
Subjects are highly identifiable from these features, and knowing
the subject alone -- with no EEG -- recovers most of the accuracy
reported under window-shuffled validation. The mechanism behind the
inflation is subject fingerprinting combined with per-subject label
imbalance, not dream-related neural information.
