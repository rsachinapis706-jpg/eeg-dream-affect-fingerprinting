==============================================================================
INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================
At 34 participants the subject-wise protocol attains 80% power
for a class separation of d = 0.00 pooled standard
deviations. The observed data show no effect at that level.
The null is therefore bounded: an effect at or above this size
would have been detected, and effects below it would not.

Power rises with participant count at fixed window count, which is
the reviewer's point: the effective sample size is the number of
participants, not the number of windows.
