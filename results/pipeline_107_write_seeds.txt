Sensitivity to initialisation was measured by repeating the second settings with three seeds, which change the head initialisation, the order of the training data and the participants held out for validation, and for the randomly initialised model the backbone as well. Pooled balanced accuracy ranges from 0.3294 to 0.3517 with pretraining (mean 0.3397, standard deviation 0.0091) and from 0.3210 to 0.3627 without (mean 0.3368, standard deviation 0.0185); the paired difference ranges from $-0.0333$ to $+0.0249$, every interval including zero, and every interval for balanced accuracy includes the 0.3333 chance level.
 Across three seeds the pretrained model ranges from 0.3294 to 0.3517 and the model trained from scratch from 0.3210 to 0.3627.
