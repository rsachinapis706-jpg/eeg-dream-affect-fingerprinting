>> Identity is carried mainly by ABSOLUTE power and the aperiodic
>> slope -- quantities determined by skull thickness, electrode
>> impedance and individual anatomy, and largely stable within a
>> person across recordings. Relative band power, which normalises
>> those away, contributes less. Any protocol that places the same
>> subject in train and test can therefore recover identity, and
>> where per-subject label distributions are skewed, identity alone
>> predicts the label.
