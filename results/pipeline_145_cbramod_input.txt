The windows given to CBraMod in Table~\ref{tab:cbramod} and for the frozen embeddings above were standardised per window and channel (median absolute value 0.627), whereas the model was pretrained on EEG in units of 100\,$\mu$V, in which these windows have median absolute value 0.040. Both analyses were therefore repeated with the windows in that unit, all other settings unchanged. The frozen embeddings then give 0.3507 against a null of 0.3410, a difference of $+0.0097$ ($d_z=0.111$, $r=0.103$; 15 participants above their null and 13 below; one-sided $p=0.323$), and PCD on them predicts on 0.0516 of windows, against 0.0594 with standardised inputs. Fine-tuned with pretraining under the second settings, at the first seed, pooled balanced accuracy is 0.3449 [0.2839, 0.4155], against 0.3517 with standardised inputs at the same seed (Table~\ref{tab:cbramod}), a paired difference of $-0.0067$ [$-0.0971$, $+0.0931$], and 4 of 34 folds predict a single class, against 8. The interval includes the 0.3333 chance level; this one run was not repeated over seeds or without pretraining. Expressing the input in the unit of pretraining therefore leaves the conclusions of this section unchanged.
