Interpretation: ingredient (ii) of the mechanism is not specific to
DEED. In most catalogued dream datasets the typical participant's
reports are concentrated in a single category, so participant identity
would be predictive of the label wherever identity is recoverable.
This does not establish that those datasets yield inflated results,
which would require access to the signals and a replication of each
study's protocol. It establishes that the precondition is common.
